# Visual World 001 — Coastal Ridge stops being a blockout

Trigger:
The full 3-hole loop is playable, but visual quality still reads as a technical blockout.

Largest meaningful gap:
The course and golfer do not yet carry enough authored physical truth to support the official LOFT art direction.

Reference bar:
The supplied LOFT brand boards:
- sculpted coastal course
- believable golf terrain
- authored cliffs / vegetation / bunkers
- stylized but human golfer
- warm editorial lighting
- quiet LOFT product typography

Rebuild:
1. Terrain from flat plane to authored elevation system.
2. Fairway / first cut / rough become separate material and geometry layers.
3. Bunkers gain depression and lips.
4. Box cliffs replaced by irregular rock shelves.
5. Native rough, shrubs and more natural pine silhouettes added.
6. Dynamic grass-detail field communicates lie near the ball.
7. Green visual geometry shares its playable physics height.
8. Lighting replaced with warm key + cool fill.
9. Character gets joint overlap, dynamic torso/pelvis rotation and golf-specific clothing detail.
10. LOFT Display / LOFT Text become the canonical CSS family names.

Current status:
TECHNICALLY PASSING.
Awaiting real-device A/B screenshots before another visual iteration.
