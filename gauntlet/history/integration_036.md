# Integration 036 — Heel attachment / shaft integrity

Retained locally: 2026-09-07. The published official v1 checkpoint remains Integration
035 on main (`55350bd`, including publication notes). No Pages setting changed.

## Reference → gap → definition

The Good Work Bag and Object Test references show a club with a deliberately shaped
heel neck, open scoring face and continuous shaft. Direct inspection in 035 instead
revealed a center-mounted oversized ferrule and paddle/flask silhouette.

Great for this pass: real iron/wedge shafts join the heel, the central scoring face is
clear, neck/shaft/grip remain connected and rigid through motion, and the existing
face center, head landmark, hands, strike timing and gameplay numbers do not change.

## Built and preserved

- Changed the shared iron/wedge hosel only, from center attachment to a heel socket.
  Its root is inside the actual forged-body triangles, not merely its bounding box.
- One shared rigid ferrule-station solver connects the existing grip endpoint to the
  new heel attachment in both the inspector and the golfer.
- Iron/wedge shaft radius is 6.25 mm; ferrule radius 8 mm, nominal length 35 mm.
  The existing 23 mm grip radius and hand contacts are unchanged.
- All 20 other-family assemblies match the frozen 035 fingerprints completely.
- All 40 non-hosel heads, materials, grip/head motion and golf profiles match the
  before-change protected fingerprint. The full 035 snapshot remains in the test;
  only the explicitly changed iron/wedge neck/shaft contract is superseded.
- No physics, scoring, cup, camera, terrain, material palette or clubface changes.

## Run → observe → critique

Live Foundation 7 Iron craft/face and Icon iron/wedge inspection show a recognizable
heel-attached club and unobstructed grooves. Equipping the Icon Sand Wedge returned
directly to a ready shot with the correct 90 YD capacity; that is the intended Equip
behavior, not a missing Close button. Browser warning/error log was empty.

The new silhouette materially beats 035. Neck credibility improves from 3.0 to 7.0,
face readability from 4.0 to 7.0 and overall actual club desirability from 4.5 to 5.4.
The flat body and very dark premium back remain obvious defects. This is not yet the
reference board's finished forging or material quality.
