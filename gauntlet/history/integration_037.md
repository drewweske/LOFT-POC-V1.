## Reference → gap → great

The Good Work Bag and zero-brand Object references use real metal shoulders, intentional
perimeter mass, cavities and restrained finish. Once 036 repaired the neck, the actual
premium iron still appeared as a dark plate with a raised rectangular badge. Metadata
calling it Pearl Ceramic did not make that black, poorly legible object convincing.

Great for this bounded pass means a real closed back shell with readable shoulder/floor
depth, ordinary solid-back construction at low level and refined recessed construction
at higher levels. No change to the scoring face, leading edge, sole, repaired neck,
grip, strike landmarks, golf numbers or unrelated club families.

## Built in the shared playable assembly

- Closed indexed metal shell made from authored outline sections: front, bevel, outer
  mass, rolled shoulder, cavity wall and recessed floor. It is not a cutout decal.
- A restrained sole/toe mass bias shapes the forging without procedural micro-noise.
- Foundation has a solid cast back; Field through Icon have increasingly refined
  physical recesses. Actual triangle-ray probes measure about 0.95 / 10.1 / 12.0 /
  13.9 / 14.3 mm center-to-sole-shoulder depth across the five grades.
- The inset follows the iron outline and sits below the real perimeter, rather than
  protruding as a rectangular block. Signature/Icon gain a curved rising sole-to-toe
  muscle instead of the old vertical badge. The topline is slimmer.
- Forged-body-only materials separate utility steel, satin metal, titanium and pearl
  treatment. Shafts, striking faces, necks, signal hardware and other families retain
  their prior materials. No inspection-only material override is used.
- Five back sections differ geometrically without labels or color. The current shell
  stays under 1,024 triangles, and complete heads retain the 14-part / 14k-triangle cap.
- Normal gameplay and Workshop still use the same cached geometry/material buffers.

## Observe → reject → improve

The first premium render immediately made the cavity and metal shoulders legible.
However, the Foundation back shaded like a molded cushion: smooth ring normals bled
into its broad planar cap. This candidate did not pass merely because the tests did.

The retained construction gives front/back cap rings their planar normals while keeping
the closed geometry and rounded shoulder transition. Live reinspection removed the
fan-shaped cushion shading. A regression assertion now samples that actual plane and
requires planar normals across at least 65 geometric samples.

The result is materially stronger than 036: shaped metal replaces the black plaque,
and the inexpensive solid back differs physically from the premium recessed object.
It is still a code-native prototype, not a finished production forging. Broad face
proportions, machined face detail and the orange weight can be refined further.
