  putts could therefore be rejected before reaching their closest approach.
  Rejection now belongs to that closest-approach encounter; hot/glancing entries
  still miss. A slow unsupported centre over the physical mouth falls; supported
  rim rests are not magnetized.
- Removed the 12 cm minimum pace distance. Adaptive short-putt pull resolution,
  inch labels, scaled pendulum and quiet tap feedback make lip finishes playable.
- Kept the putting view through contact; cup sound meets the physical drop.
- A second finger cancels provisional stroke intent and keeps pinch zoom usable.
- A holed result posts its score before the HUD refresh and reads COMPLETE / HOLED,
  not a fictitious next stroke or remaining foot.
