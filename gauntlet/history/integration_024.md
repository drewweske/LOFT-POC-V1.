# Integration 024 — LOFT Ridge House Destination V1

## Outcome

The small beige clubhouse placeholder is now a recognizable coastal destination
object. It anchors the inland side of the route with a stepped silhouette and an
honest stone / plaster / timber / glass material hierarchy while staying safely
outside maintained play.

## Built

- deep grounded stone plinth and retaining landing
- broad warm clubhouse body with a taller west volume and lower east pavilion
- two nested gabled roof rhythms plus a restrained veranda canopy
- wrap terrace, landing steps and side deck
- instanced structural posts, fascia, rails and slats
- instanced dark coastal-glass window and entry family
- integrated stone chimney and exactly one restrained entry signal
- merged material families and instancing to retain detail at seven total draws
- frozen `LOFT_RIDGE_HOUSE_V1` budget / placement contract and executable validator

## Protected contracts

- complete 18.5 × 14 m footprint resolves to rough
- minimum sampled fairway clearance is approximately 15.7 m
- the foundation spans the sampled 0.74 m terrain variation without floating
- root and every visible descendant are explicitly non-playable
- no building collider or alternative terrain height was introduced
- exact terrain, golf, ball, cup, scoring, map, camera and round logic are unchanged

## Before / after

Before: one 10.8 m beige box, a smaller box, one roof slab, four individual window
planes and thin disconnected rails read as a temporary hut.

After: the course overview has a long, stepped Ridge House with a tower-like west
volume, broad dark roof line, visible window cadence, chimney and wrap veranda. It
reads as a place with a program rather than a decorative block.

## Performance

- 11 windows / entries and 23 timber-frame elements
- one signal object
- all limits substantially inside the 12,000-triangle hard ceiling

## Honest result

The Ridge House now earns its place in the skyline and decisively improves the old
placeholder at negligible render cost. It remains a code-native distant landmark,
not a claim of explorable production architecture. At normal gameplay distance the
silhouette and material rhythm matter more than invisible interior detail.
